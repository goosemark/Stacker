# Stacker: Tower Climb Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Stacker: Tower Climb does not collect personal data or track you. The game works offline and does not require an account.

Game progress, settings, scores, daily records, and saved Practice towers are stored on your device. The app does not send this information to the developer or an analytics service.

If you choose to share a tower image, iOS presents its share sheet. The destination you select handles what you share under its own privacy practices. Sharing is optional.

Stacker has no advertising or in-app purchases. The app does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/goosemark/Stacker/issues.

This policy describes version 1.0 of Stacker: Tower Climb. If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated.
